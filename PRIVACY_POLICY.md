## Summary at a Glance

- **100% Offline:** TarkovRadar does not connect to any servers, cloud backends, or remote APIs.
- **Zero Personal Data:** We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.
- **No Tracking or Advertising:** No sign-in required, no cookies, no advertising SDKs, and no analytics.
- **Local Device Storage Only:** Tactical map markers, floor notes, and extract checklist states remain strictly in private local SQLite (Room) storage on your Android device.

---

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

TarkovRadar ("the App") operates entirely on-device as an offline tactical companion guide for Escape From Tarkov players. We do not collect, gather, log, transmit, or sell:

- **Personal Identifiers:** Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
- **Device Identifiers:** IMEI, Android Advertising ID (AAID), MAC address, or serial numbers.
- **Location Data:** GPS, Wi-Fi location, or network cell tower positioning.
- **Diagnostic & Usage Data:** Automated crash logs, usage statistics, or telemetry.

---

## 2. Local Data Storage & User Control

The App utilizes local Android Room (SQLite) storage solely to save custom tactical markers, floor notes, and extraction checklist progress placed by the user.

- All data remains on your physical device at all times and is never synchronized to cloud servers.
- You can permanently erase all stored application data at any time via standard Android settings:  
  **Settings > Apps > TarkovRadar > Storage & Cache > Clear Storage**.

---

## 3. Third-Party Libraries and SDKs

TarkovRadar does not integrate third-party advertising networks (e.g. Google AdMob), analytics services (e.g. Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics), social media tracking SDKs, or external marketing trackers.
