Privacy Policy & Security Disclosure

Privacy Policy for TarkovRadar

Effective Date: October 31, 2026 • Version 5.0 (Zero-Telemetry Architecture)
Summary at a Glance
  • 100% Offline: TarkovRadar does not connect to any servers, backends, or APIs.
  • Zero Personal Data: We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information.
  • No Accounts or Tracking: No sign-in required, no cookies, no advertising SDKs, and no analytics.
  • Local Device Storage Only: Tactical map markers and extraction checklists are kept solely in private local SQLite storage on your Android device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

TarkovRadar ("the App") operates entirely on-device as an offline tactical companion guide for Escape From Tarkov players. We do not collect, gather, log, or track:

• Personal Identifiers (such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or gamer tags)
• Device Identifiers (such as IMEI, Android Advertising ID, or MAC address)
• Geolocation or precise physical location data
• Network telemetry or diagnostic crash data

2. Local Data Storage & User Control

The App utilizes local Android Room (SQLite) storage solely to save custom tactical markers, floor notes, and extract checklist states placed by the user. This data remains on your physical device at all times and is never synchronized to cloud servers.

Users can permanently delete all locally stored data at any time through standard Android system settings: Settings > Apps > TarkovRadar > Storage & Cache > Clear Storage.

3. Third-Party Libraries and SDKs

TarkovRadar does not integrate third-party advertising networks (e.g. AdMob), analytics services (e.g. Google Analytics / Firebase), social media tracking SDKs, or external marketing trackers. The application contains zero promotional trackers.

4. Permissions Usage

TarkovRadar requires zero sensitive Android permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or photo gallery.

5. Children's Privacy

TarkovRadar does not address anyone under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If our privacy practices change, this policy will be updated accordingly. Continued use of the application signifies acceptance of any updated terms.
